# Presentation timing and design

The refreshed deck has eight slides. Aim for five minutes of explanation, seven
minutes of demonstration and a short feedback discussion. Use
[speaker notes](project-overview-speaker-notes.md),
[practice script](project-overview-read-through-script.md) and
[run-of-show](final-demo-run-of-show.md).

1. FIELDWORK and the environment goal.
2. Backend foundation and separate evidence lanes.
3. User-confirmed chair room workflow.
4. Implemented description-to-asset/MCP path.
5. Evidence levels: live baseline, native fixture and offline tests.
6. Remaining SSH/couch/engine/recording checks.
7. Explicit scope and validation limits.
8. Whole-result review and feedback.

Keep slides sparse and readable. Show complete saved layouts/results, not long
terminal output. Preserve contrast, whitespace and a visible distinction between
implemented software and verified live results. Do not use completion percentages
or imply new evidence from a refreshed document date.

Rebuild the deck with `python docs/presentations/build_pdf.py`. The report and
four-file packet are separate exports; see [reports](../reports/README.md).
